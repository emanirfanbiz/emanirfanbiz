### 🤖 AI Chatbot Interaction Effectiveness
*Human-computer interaction · 2025–2026*

What makes people trust, and enjoy, talking to an AI chatbot? I set out to answer that with a systematic review of **55 peer-reviewed papers**, turning them into a dataset of 55 rows and 24 features and putting **six ML/DL models** head to head. Random Forest came out on top at **72.73% accuracy**, and the data pointed to **anthropomorphism, personalization and trust** as the design factors that matter most.

### 👁️ Face Recognition & Depth Verification
*Computer vision · 2025–2026*

An attendance system that doesn't just recognize a face, it checks that the face is **real**. It pairs **Haar Cascade** detection and **LBPH** recognition with **SIFT, HOG, Harris and Canny** feature extraction, measures depth with **stereo triangulation**, and exposes photo spoofing through **Laplacian-variance** analysis.
